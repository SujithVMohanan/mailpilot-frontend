Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 18, 2026

Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

Mail Pilot's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

1. Overview & Purpose

Mail Pilot is a web-based email productivity application designed to help users organize, categorize, and automate their Gmail messages through custom filtering rules.

This Privacy Policy explains how Mail Pilot collects, uses, protects, and manages your personal information and Google user data when you access our application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum data required to execute your configured email automation workflows:

  • Account Information: When you register an account, we collect your email address and a securely salted password hash.
  • Google OAuth 2.0 Credentials: When you authorize Gmail integration, Google provides an OAuth access token, refresh token, and your email address. We do not receive or store your Google account password.
  • Email Headers & Content: To evaluate your automated filtering rules, we synchronize message identifiers, sender address, recipient address, subject, timestamp, label IDs, snippet, and message body text.
  • Automation Rules & Execution Logs: We store the conditions you configure (e.g., "Subject contains Invoice") and the audit history of executed actions.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to provide the core service:

  • Evaluating configured rules against stored messages locally in our Python rule engine.
  • Executing actions you have explicitly configured, such as marking emails as read/unread or moving them to designated labels via the Gmail API.
  • Presenting your synchronized inbox, automation statistics, and execution history on your personal dashboard.
  • Protecting the security and multi-tenant isolation of your account.

4. Strict Limitations on Google User Data (Limited Use)

In accordance with Google's Limited Use requirements:

✓ Zero Advertising
We never use or transfer your email content or metadata for serving advertisements or marketing.
✓ No Selling of Data
We never sell, rent, license, or monetize your personal information or email data to third parties.
✓ No AI/ML Training
Your email data is never used to train generalized artificial intelligence or large language models.
✓ No Human Reading
No humans read your email messages unless you give explicit permission for technical support or required by law.

5. Data Security & Encryption

We apply enterprise-grade safeguards to keep your data secure:

  • Encryption at Rest: OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest in our database using strong symmetric cryptography (`EncryptedTextField`).
  • Encryption in Transit: All data transferred between your browser, our servers, and Google APIs is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Strict Tenancy: Each user's data is strictly partitioned so users can only ever view and modify their own emails and rules.

6. Data Retention, Disconnect & Deletion

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • Disconnecting from Mail Pilot: You can disconnect your Gmail account at any time with a single click from the Dashboard. Disconnecting immediately revokes our access and purges all stored tokens.
  • Revoking Access via Google: You can revoke Mail Pilot's permissions at any time through your Google Account Security Settings.
  • Complete Account Deletion: You can request full deletion of your user account and all synchronized email records by contacting our support team.
